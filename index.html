Sapporo & the cool north

One base in Sapporo, ten slow nights, and a fan of day trips into Hokkaido's lakes, gorges, hot springs and hills — chasing summer's coolest, least-humid corners. Trains only.

How the 10 days could flow

A tentative shape, not a lock — dates shift with flights. Built for "active but lazy": ~5 day trips threaded between slow Sapporo days, with the three real hauls (Shikotsu · Furano · Toya) never back-to-back. Tap any linked day to jump to its gallery.

Arrive

Arrive — converge on Sapporo

Aryan lands New Chitose from Toronto (the long pole); Jean & Saurav likely in earlier. Check in once, unpack for 10 nights. Easy first night: Susukino dinner + a cold Sapporo Classic. Nothing strenuous.

Sapporo

Sapporo, slowly

Jetlag-friendly city day. Nijo Market breakfast, Odori Park & TV Tower, red-brick Akarenga, then Mt Moiwa for the sunset night view. Find your coffee spot and your izakaya.

35-min train. Canal, Sakaimachi street, glass-blowing, sushi-counter lunch, soft serve. Half-to-full day, back whenever. The gentlest day trip on the board.

HAUL #1. Japan's clearest lake: clear-bottom kayak over the 'Shikotsu blue', himemasu lunch, lakeside onsen. Coolest, crispest air of the trip — placed early while everyone's fresh.

Slow Sapporo + Jozankei half-day

Recovery day. Sleep in, soup curry, Beer Museum or Shiroi Koibito Park — or bolt on Jozankei (Sapporo's backyard onsen gorge, ~60 min) for an afternoon soak and the Futami bridge. Low-energy by design.

HAUL #2, the biggest. Lavender Express ~2 hr. Blue Pond, Shirahige Falls, Biei patchwork hills by e-bike, Farm Tomita flowers, Furano melon. ⚠️ Pre-book the e-bike / taxi-tour / Twinkle Bus — farm buses are sparse.

Fly New Chitose → Haneda (~1h45) or Tohoku Shinkansen if you want the ride. Into Tokyo for 3 nights (Aug 26–28) in new-to-you neighborhoods, then everyone flies home Sat Aug 29.

Considered, but too far by train

Beautiful spots we ruled out for a strict single base on public transit — noted so you know we checked.

Asahidake / Daisetsuzan

The coolest air in Japan (summit 10–15°C) — but ~4.5–5 hrs each way and only works as an overnight. Breaks the single-base rule.

Mt Yotei / Niseko

The 'Ezo Fuji' is stunning, but ~3.5 hrs and a car-trap — trailheads and farms are unreachable without driving.

Sounkyo Gorge

Waterfalls and columnar cliffs, but ~3.5 hrs each way — better as an Asahikawa overnight than a day trip.
